About

Hi, I’m Waldemar - a Scala & Big Data engineer from Warsaw with 15+ years of commercial experience in banking, fintech and the public sector.

This is my tech blog: software development and everything around it - tools, hardware, operating systems and the way we work.

Outside of work I’m a father and a motorcyclist ;)

Scala for the win

Scala is the best JVM language out there, and it has been my main language for over 10 years. It runs on the JVM, in web browsers and natively. Lately I spend my open source time on Scala tooling for HOCON configuration files.
