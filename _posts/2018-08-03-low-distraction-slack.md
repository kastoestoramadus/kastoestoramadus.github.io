## Who is this post for?
- Your Slack workspace is big, with a lot of busy channels.
- You don't react to most messages that aren't direct or don't use @here or @channel.
- You read too many messages, which is counterproductive.

Too many notifications make you unresponsive to what has actual value.

## Solution
Slack has about 4 priority levels you can set for a channel:
1. Starred - for your obligations and the topics you love to read about.
2. Normal - for things that aren't very disturbing (a slow source or something quite important).
3. Muted - for things that aren't your obligations, but that you sometimes need or like to skim once a week or month.
4. Not listed - for channels not even worth seeing the name of.

Using all of them, you get a clean sidebar: you won't miss anything important and you won't be distracted by small productivity killers.
