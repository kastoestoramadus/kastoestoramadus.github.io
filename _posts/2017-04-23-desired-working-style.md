I would like to get accurate offers that match my profile. To help you with that, please read about the working styles I prefer.

## 0. Regular contract
I'm still interested, but it has a lower priority.

## I. Intensive short contract
100% on-site. A very intensive and very well paid contract, anywhere on the globe. If I'm busy, mention this style in your first message.

## II. Very intensive: 3 weeks on, 1 week off
100% on-site. Longer contracts preferred, no remote work required. My favourite style. Permanent roles possible.

For three weeks I'm close to your workplace. I do everything needed, I come early and leave late. If possible, I also work on the 6th day of the week. I accumulate all this overtime to get "a paid week off". I also need a break during the day to stay creative (no longer than 30 minutes, without any interaction).

- If there aren't enough overtime hours, I accept unpaid days off (3 per month at most).
- If it's easier to account for, instead of paid days off I agree to a higher daily rate with an unpaid week off.

## III. Partially remote work
1-3 weeks on-site, the rest remotely. Permanent roles possible.

## IV. Full-time remote work
As commonly known.

## Any mix of the above styles

## My commitment
- During my days off I commit to being available in case my context is needed. I will check messages and advise if needed.
- A remote intervention needs to be scheduled, so that I have my full equipment with me.
