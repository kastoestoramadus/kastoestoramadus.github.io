In theory, the European Union has a common language - English. In practice, there is no common language for European culture.

I'm Polish. I speak Polish, English and a bit of German. I don't personally know any non-English-speaking culture of Europe. I can't touch German culture, I can't touch French culture. Everything I read is in English or Polish.

I read about niche topics in articles from the USA, the UK, Australia and India. Thanks to those countries I grow my knowledge about motorcycles, ecology, technology, life and much more. Those countries are so far away from each other, yet they exchange culture much better than European countries that are so close to each other...

There is no language I can write in to be understood both by my own nation and by our neighbours. The same goes for them. It makes me very sad. If you are Polish, you write in Polish to become popular in Poland - obviously. But by doing so, you limit your reach and the development of European culture.

Different languages have a huge impact on the alienation of Europe. In the long term, good culture is the highest value we can create. No law can substitute for culture.

Why do so many more innovations and startups happen in the USA and so few in Europe?

Learning a new language well enough to easily express subtle thoughts is too big an effort for an average person. I see the problem. Do you see it too?

Thank you for your attention,
Waldek
