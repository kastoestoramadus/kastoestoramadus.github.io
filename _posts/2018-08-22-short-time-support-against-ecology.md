## How long is your phone supported?
Why do I even ask? The easiest way to hack a device is to use well-known exploits. Every published update reveals holes that unpatched phones still have! Hacking an up-to-date system is simply too expensive for a regular hacker.

## So... how long is your tablet supported?
* Cheap Androids - not at all
* Mid-range Androids - 1 year
* Premium Androids - 2 years
* Google's own Androids (and some premium ones, with security patches only) - 3 years
* iPhone/iPad - 3 years of all updates, plus 2 more years of security updates. But after a few years the updates seem to slow the device down...
* Chrome OS / Chromebooks - 5 years
* Windows 10 - unlimited?

And remember: the clock starts at the premiere, not on the day you buy the device!

## What to do with an unsupported device?
* Use it for a DIY project or install custom firmware? Who does that - less than 1% of people?
* Sell it to a poorer country? And what will they do with it then?
* Throw it away? A waste of resources... The hardware is probably still good enough for most of our needs.

The whole mobile industry seems to be designed against ecology!

## My problem
I wanted a tablet that lasts. I have a Samsung Galaxy Note 10.1 2014 Edition - a premium device, 5 years old and unsupported... Now I regret the money. The screen is still gorgeous, much better than in the basic 9.7-inch iPad from 2018! But without updates I can do less and less with it, and nothing related to banking...

I replace only my phone every 1-3 years. Why replace a tablet that often? I don't use it enough to wear it out.

I've checked the new Android tablets: their support is still short. Google seems to have forgotten about Android tablets (no new Google tablet for years)... in favour of Chrome OS on Chromebooks and 2-in-1 tablets! Those run Android apps too, work better as tablets and get 5 years of support!

## But what can you do with Chrome OS?
Everything popular... but it's still more a netbook than a tablet. I develop software in Scala, and a JDK or an IDE isn't possible there. Chrome OS is for cloud apps, and I need some unpopular local ones.

## Windows 10?
It has the longest support possible! It lacks apps, but I could work on it and emulate Android games... Still, it's Windows, which needs more care to work like Linux.

## A really good solution doesn't exist
For now I'll stick with a Linux laptop... and wait for a cheap alternative to the Surface Pro with 16 GB of RAM and 4 cores (why? Better screen proportions for reading, and a portrait mode).

Do you have any thoughts or suggestions? Feel free to comment.

And please make a change: look for devices with long-term security support.
