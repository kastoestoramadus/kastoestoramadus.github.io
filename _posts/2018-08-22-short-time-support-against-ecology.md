## How long is the support for your phone?
Why do I even ask? Did you know that the easiest way to hack a device is to use already well-known exploits? Every published update reveals holes that unpatched phones still have! Hacking an up-to-date system is just too expensive for a regular hacker.

## So... how long is the support for your tablet?
* Cheap Androids - not supported at all
* Mid-range Androids - 1 year
* Premium Android smartphones - 2 years
* Google Android smartphones (and some premium ones with security-only patches) - 3 years
* iPhone/iPad - 3 years of all updates, plus 2 more years of security updates. But after a few years the updates seem to slow your phone down...
* Chrome OS / Chromebooks - 5 years
* Windows 10 - unlimited?

And remember: the clock ticks from the premiere, not from the day you buy it!

## What to do with an unsupported device?
* Use it for a DIY project or load custom firmware? Who does that? Less than 1% of the population?
* Sell it to a poorer country? And what will they do with it then?
* Throw it away? Wasted resources... The hardware is probably still strong enough for most of our activities.

The whole mobile industry seems to be designed against ecology!

## What is my problem
I wanted a long-lasting tablet. I now have a Samsung Galaxy Note 10.1 2014 Edition - a premium, 5-year-old, unsupported device... Now I regret the money. The screen is still gorgeous, much better than in the basic 9.7-inch iPad from 2018! Without support I can do less and less with it, and nothing banking-related...

I change only my phones every 1-3 years. A tablet... why change it so often? I don't use it enough to wear it out.

I've checked the new Android tablets. They still get short support. Google seems to have forgotten about Android on tablets (no new Google tablet for years)... in favour of Chrome OS, Chromebooks and 2-in-1 tablets! They still run Android apps, they are better designed for tablets and they get 5 years of support!

## But what can you do with Chrome OS?
Everything popular... but it's still more a netbook than a tablet. Personally, I do Scala software development, and a JDK or an IDE isn't possible there. Chrome OS is for cloud apps, and I need some unpopular local apps.

## Windows 10?
The support period is the longest possible! It lacks apps, but I could work on it and emulate Android games... Still, it's Windows, which needs more care to work like Linux.

## A very good solution does not exist
For now I'll stick with a laptop running Linux... while waiting for a cheap 16 GB, 4-core alternative to the Surface Pro (why? Better screen proportions for reading and a portrait mode).

Do you have any thoughts or suggestions? Feel free to comment.

And please make a change: look for devices with long-term security support.
